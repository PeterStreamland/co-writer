# Sample credits

Both sample sets are licensed under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

- `piano/`: Salamander Grand Piano V3 (a Yamaha C5) by Alexander Holm, as published in the [Tone.js audio repository](https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander). Notes A1 to C7, every minor third, unchanged.
- `guitar/`: clean electric guitar from [tonejs-instruments](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments) by Nicholas Brosowsky. Notes E2 to C6, unchanged.
